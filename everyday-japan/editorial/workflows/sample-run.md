# Sample Run — Convenience Store Article (Illustrative)

This walkthrough shows how the pipeline would have produced the reference article. Artifacts below are abbreviated for documentation — the full reference lives in `reference/`.

## 1. Topic

**Title:** Why Japanese Convenience Stores Are So Different From The Rest Of The World  
**Target keyword:** Japanese convenience store  
**Intent:** informational  
**Priority:** high  
**Category:** Everyday Life / Food

Reader questions included: Why does konbini food feel different? Can tourists use ATMs? Why are they everywhere?

## 2. Research

Collected:

- Local uses (breakfast, late work, small household needs)
- Service bundle (ATM, printing, delivery, bill pay)
- Seasonal product culture
- Density / walking / storage lifestyle conditions
- Disaster-infrastructure role
- Misconception: "convenience store food is low quality"

Flagged for verification: international ATM acceptance by chain/card, exact service availability by chain.

## 3. Outline

Eight-part structure:

1. First visitor scene → "it's just a convenience store?"
2. Question: why does it feel different?
3. Local perspective: neighborhood tool, not destination
4. Background: convenience as removing small difficulties
5. Examples: egg sandwich, seasons, invisible maintenance, omotenashi micro-actions, density
6. Visitor tips + emergency context
7. FAQ
8. Closing: visitor sees culture; local sees normal stop

## 4. Writing

Produced ~magazine-length draft with short paragraphs, concrete scenes, WHY explanations, FAQ, and closing insight — without hype.

## 5. SEO

- Title / meta / slug around Japanese convenience store / konbini
- Tags and FAQ schema
- Related future topics: trains, vending machines, sumimasen

## 6. Fact Check

Verified general lifestyle claims; flagged chain-specific service differences and ATM caveats already softened in copy ("availability depends…").

## 7. Human Review

Checklist completed: natural tone, local perspective, cultural explanation, no stereotypes, SEO ready, images needed (store exterior, egg sandwich, seasonal shelf — illustrative).

## 8. Publish

Article published as editorial reference standard.
