## Facts

- Many buildings post quiet-hour / laundry guidance in house rules, not a single national curfew law.
- Wooden apaato often more acoustically leaky than many RC mansion buildings; variation is large.
- Souon (騒音) is the everyday complaint word.
- Children are a known social exception and conflict.
- Slippers: cleanliness first, noise as side effect.
