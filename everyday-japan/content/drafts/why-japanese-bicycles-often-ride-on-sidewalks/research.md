## Facts

- Legal default: bicycles on the road.
- Exceptions: sidewalk-permitted signs; age/disability; unavoidable roadway danger.
- Pedestrians have priority on sidewalks when bikes are there.

See `content/articles/why-japanese-bicycles-often-ride-on-sidewalks/fact-check.json` for claim table.

## Common Misconceptions

- Japan assigns bikes to sidewalks as the main rule
- No danger to pedestrians
- Every sidewalk is a bike lane
