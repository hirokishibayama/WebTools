# Seven Review Skills — checklists

Act as each specialist in turn (or in parallel reasoning). Find concrete problems and suggestions, not only scores.

Score bands: 90–100 Excellent · 80–89 Good · 70–79 Needs Improvement · 60–69 Major Revision · 0–59 Rewrite  
Skill status: ≥80 `pass` · 60–79 `revise` · <60 or any critical `fail`

---

## 1. Reader Experience → `reader.json` (`reviewer`: `reader-experience`)

Foreign readers: clear, engaging, worth finishing?

- Opening question clear?
- Why read this article?
- Explanation too hard?
- Repetition?
- Mid-article dropout points?
- Enough concrete examples?
- “Aha” moment?
- Useful when visiting Japan?
- Reason to read to the end?

---

## 2. Japanese Perspective → `japanese-perspective.json`

Everyday Japan local lens — not generic tourism copy.

- Everyday Japanese sense explained?
- Not foreigner-facing platitudes?
- What locals take for granted made visible?
- Habit background explained?
- Tied to values / life / society?
- No stereotypes?
- No invented first-person Japanese anecdotes?
- No “Japanese people think…” over-generalization?

If Wikipedia-like, propose specific improvements.

---

## 3. Cultural Accuracy → `cultural-accuracy.json`

Misunderstanding, stereotypes, over-generalization.

- Facts / history accurate?
- Regional / generational variation ignored?
- “All Japanese people…”?
- Outdated as if current?
- Mystified / “Japan is unique” laziness?
- Over-polite / over-clean national myth?

---

## 4. Fact & Source → `fact-source.json`

Numbers, dates, laws, institutions, history, stats, surveys, proper nouns, causation, “it is said…”.

Buckets: `verified` · `questionable` · `unsupported` · `needs_source`

Separate fact from speculation. Prefer hedges over false certainty.

---

## 5. SEO & Search Intent → `seo.json` (`reviewer`: `seo-search-intent`)

Answer the searcher’s real question — no keyword stuffing.

- Intent clear? Title / intro match?
- H2/H3 natural?
- Keywords natural?
- Related questions?
- Off-intent digressions too long?

Do **not** make prose unnatural for SEO. Low SEO alone ≠ full rewrite.

---

## 6. English Naturalness → `english.json` (`reviewer`: `english-naturalness`)

Native-sounding editor, not “AI explaining Japan.”

Flag: translationese, over-formal, repeated syntax, vague wording, dramatic tone, stock AI lines such as:

- "Japan is a country where…"
- "In many ways…"
- "This unique aspect of Japanese culture…"
- "For visitors, this can be surprising…"
- "At first glance…"
- "It may seem strange, but…"

Replace with concrete scenes.

---

## 7. Editorial Originality → `originality.json` (`reviewer`: `editorial-originality`)

Everyday Japan value — not a clone of other Japan sites.

- Not Wikipedia / tourism-site clone?
- Not cliché conclusion?
- Japanese-side supplement / surprising angle?
- Info readers cannot get elsewhere?
- Concrete observation / comparison / examples?

“Correct only” is not enough — need “I didn’t know that.”
